Squeek Tester one safe test

Unofficial · Sepolia (testnet) · you sign, nothing is automatic
What this does

Sends one test transaction to the official SQK token on Sepolia: approve(your own address, 0). The amount is zero and the spender is you, so no funds move and nothing can be spent. It only costs a tiny amount of testnet gas (free test ETH from a faucet).

Reading the contract…

1 · Connect your wallet

2 · Free check (nothing is sent)

3 · Send the test transaction (once)

Official addresses come from the official deployment record. This page never asks for a key or a seed phrase and never sends anything without your wallet confirmation. One transaction is enough: it does not retry, and it will not let you send twice from this browser.
